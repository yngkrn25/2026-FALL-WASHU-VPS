READING RESPONSE 1

READING RESPONSE FOR: "Toph Tucker and Jasmine Lee on Why Restaurant Websites Are Good and We’re All Going to Miss Them"

I never really paid much attention to restaurant websites because I usually just use Google Maps or Instagram to find the menu and hours. What I found interesting in this reading was how an older or less polished website can actually show more personality than a modern website made from a template. I normally prefer websites that are simple and organized, so I did not expect to like some of the examples as much as I did. It also made me think about how tools like Squarespace make creating a website much easier, but at the same time they can make a lot of websites start to look similar. Since I am not very comfortable with coding myself, I understand why people would choose an easier tool, but I can also see how knowing how to code gives you more freedom to make something feel personal. I especially liked the idea that a restaurant website can show the atmosphere of the actual restaurant before you even visit it.
