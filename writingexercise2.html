WRITING EXERCISE 2

WRITING EXERCISE FOR:

What is a human-made creation that brings out the best in you? Why?

One human-made creation that brings out the best in me is the automobile, especially the cars in my family's collection.

Cars have always been an important part of my life because they connect me with my family and some of my favorite memories.

Whenever I see or drive a car that I really like, I feel excited, relaxed, and happy. It helps me forget about stress and focus on something I genuinely enjoy.

I also appreciate the design, engineering, and small details that make each car unique. This inspires my creativity and encourages me to pay more attention to design in my own work.

For me, cars are more than just transportation. They represent my passion, my family, and the things that make me happy.
