MEDIA RESPONSE FOR:

"Chia Amisola: There Are No Gardens on the Internet"

What I found most interesting about There Are No Gardens on the Internet was how it made me explore instead of just scroll through a normal website.

I liked that I had to click around, find things, and slowly figure out what was happening instead of being told exactly where to go.

The idea of using a garden as a metaphor for the internet was also interesting because a garden feels organized and controlled, while the internet can be much more random and unpredictable.

I also liked how the visuals and interactions made the website feel more like a small world or game than a webpage.

It made me think about how the structure of a website can completely change the way we experience the information inside it.
