READING RESPONSE 4

READING RESPONSE FOR:

"The Forbidden Zone"

What stood out to me most in The Forbidden Zone was Damon Zucconi’s idea that if you build a container for something, you will eventually find things to fill it with.

I liked this because it made me think of a website as more than just a place to display finished work. It can actually shape what you collect, what you notice, and even what you decide to make.

I also found it interesting that he saves images, texts, and ideas even when he does not know if they will ever become useful.

A lot of important things in his work seem to come from random encounters rather than from searching for one specific thing.

This connected well with the shimmering points exercise because I also noticed that some of the things I chose were things I had remembered for years without really knowing why.

The reading made me think that collecting those things can be part of the creative process itself, not just preparation for the final work.
