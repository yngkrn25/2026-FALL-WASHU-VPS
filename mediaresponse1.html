MEDIA RESPONSE 1

MEDIA RESPONSE FOR:

"ENO — A FILM BY GARY HUSTWIT"

I thought Eno was interesting because it did not feel like a normal documentary.

The way the film used different clips, sounds, and changing sequences made it feel more personal and unpredictable.

I also liked how calm and thoughtful Brian Eno seemed when he talked about creativity, music, and making art.

It felt like he was more interested in experimenting and discovering something new than trying to control every result.

The film made me think that art does not always need to have one fixed meaning or one perfect final version.

Sometimes the process, accidents, and unexpected changes can be just as important as the final work.
