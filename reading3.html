READING RESPONSE 3

READING RESPONSE FOR: "My website is a shifting house next to a river of knowledge. What could yours be?"

What I found interesting about this reading was the idea that a website does not have to just be a clean and finished page that shows information. I usually think of websites as something that should be organized and easy to use, so comparing them to a room, garden, plant, or even a puddle felt very different to me. I liked the idea that a website can keep changing over time instead of ever being completely finished. If I had to describe my own website as something, I think it would be more like a garage or a showroom where I could keep changing what is inside depending on what I am interested in at the time. I also think making your own website gives you much more control over how people experience it, compared to using something like Instagram or another platform where the structure is already decided for you.
