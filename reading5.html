READING RESPONSE FOR:

"The Coming Age of Calm Technology"

I found it interesting that we can notice things in the background without actively thinking about them.

The example of hearing a car engine while driving was especially interesting to me.

Normally, we do not focus on the engine sound, but we immediately notice when something sounds wrong.

This made me think about how technology can provide useful information without constantly distracting us.

I also connected this idea to iPhone StandBy Mode.

It allows us to see the time and other basic information without having to constantly interact with our phones.

For this project, I want to explore how time can be displayed in a simple way that feels useful and calm.
