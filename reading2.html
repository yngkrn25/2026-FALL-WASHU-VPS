READING RESPONSE 2

READING RESPONSE FOR:

"The New Turing Test"

I thought the idea of a “The New Turing Test" was interesting because I usually think about AI in terms of whether it can imitate a human well enough.

This reading made me think more about the opposite question, which is how we can make our own work feel more human as AI gets better. I use AI pretty often, so I do not think using AI automatically makes something bad or less creative.

I think it depends more on how much of your own thinking and personality is still in the final result.

Especially in design, something can look very polished but still feel generic or like it could have been made by anyone.

I also liked the idea that imperfections can actually make work feel more personal, because usually I think of mistakes as something that should always be fixed.
