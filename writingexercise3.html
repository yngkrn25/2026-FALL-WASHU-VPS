WRITING EXERCISE 3

WRITING EXERCISE FOR:

When do you cherish the slow or hard way of doing something? Why?

When I work on personal design projects, I sometimes prefer doing things the slower and harder way instead of using ready-made templates.

For example, when I was creating my family's car collection website, I spent a lot of time adjusting small details like images, spacing, and animations.

Even though using a website builder would have been much easier, I wanted to create something that matched my original idea as closely as possible.

The process was sometimes frustrating, but I felt more satisfied when I finally got things to look the way I wanted.

I think doing things the harder way can help us become more creative and appreciate the work we put into something.

This also made me realize that technology should not always focus on making everything faster. It should also give people the freedom to explore their own ideas and enjoy the creative process.
